# Computer Lab Rules

## 1. Respect the Equipment

- **Handle with Care:** Computers and monitors are delicate. Don't bang on the keyboard or move the monitor roughly.
- **No Food or Drink:** Keep liquids away from the computers to prevent spills that can damage the electronics.
- **Keep it Clean:** If you spill something, clean it up immediately. If you can't, tell your teacher.

## 2. Keep it Tidy

- **Cable Management:** Don't unplug cables unless you know what you're doing. If a cable is loose, tell your teacher.
- **Clean Workspace:** Keep your desk clear of unnecessary items. Only have what you need for your lesson.
- **Proper Shutdown:** Always shut down the computer properly when you're done. Don't just turn off the power strip.

## 3. Use it for Learning

- **Stay on Task:** Use the computer for the assigned lesson only. No gaming or social media unless it's part of the lesson.
- **Ask for Help:** If you don't know how to do something, ask your teacher. Don't guess or mess with settings you don't understand.
- **Save Your Work:** Save your work to your folder or cloud storage. Don't save it to the desktop.

## 4. Be a Good Lab Citizen

- **Share the Equipment:** If you're working in a group, make sure everyone gets a chance to use the computer.
- **Be Quiet:** Keep your voice down so you don't disturb others who are working.
- **Help Each Other:** If you see someone struggling, offer to help them (but don't take over their computer).

## 5. Safety First

- **Report Problems:** If you see something unsafe (like a frayed cable or a computer that's making strange noises), tell your teacher immediately.
- **Don't Open Anything:** Never open the computer case or try to fix hardware issues yourself.
- **Wash Your Hands:** Wash your hands after using the computers, especially if you have a cold or flu.
