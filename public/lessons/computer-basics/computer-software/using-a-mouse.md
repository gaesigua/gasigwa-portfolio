# Using a Mouse

## 1. Holding the Mouse

- **Palm Grip:** Rest your palm on the back of the mouse.
- **Fingers:** Place your index finger on the left-click button and your middle finger on the right-click button.
- **Thumb:** Your thumb should rest on the side of the mouse for support.

## 2. Moving the Mouse

- **Slide:** Move the mouse smoothly across a flat surface.
- **Wrist Movement:** Use your wrist to make small adjustments.
- **Arm Movement:** Use your whole arm for large movements across the screen.

## 3. Clicking

- **Left-Click:** Press the left button once quickly. This is used to select items or open menus.
- **Right-Click:** Press the right button once quickly. This opens a context menu with more options.
- **Double-Click:** Click the left button twice quickly. This is used to open files or applications.

## 4. Drag and Drop

- **Click and Hold:** Click and hold the left mouse button.
- **Drag:** Move the mouse while still holding the button down.
- **Release:** Release the button to drop the item in the new location.

## 5. Using the Scroll Wheel

- **Scroll Up:** Roll the wheel towards you to move the page up.
- **Scroll Down:** Roll the wheel away from you to move the page down.
- **Middle-Click:** Press the scroll wheel like a button to open links in new tabs.
