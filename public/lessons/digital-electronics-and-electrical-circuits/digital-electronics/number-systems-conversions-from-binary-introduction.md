# Converting FROM BINARY

In this lesson, let's learn how to convert numbers from **Binary** to other number systems (**Decimal**, **Octal**, **Hexadecimal**).

## Why Convert FROM Binary?

We convert **from** binary for two main reasons:

1. **Machine-Friendly to Human-Friendly**: Computers understand binary, but humans find decimal easier to read and write.
2. **Compact Representation**: Hexadecimal (base 16) is much shorter than binary for representing the same value.
