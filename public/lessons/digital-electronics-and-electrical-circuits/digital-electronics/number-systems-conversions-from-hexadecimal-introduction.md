# Converting FROM HEXADECIMAL

In the next lessons, let's learn how to convert numbers from **Hexadecimal** to other number systems (**Decimal**, **Binary**, **Octal**).

## Why Convert FROM Hexadecimal?

We convert **from** hexadecimal for two main reasons:

1. **Human-Friendly to Machine-Friendly**: Computers understand binary, but humans find decimal easier to read and write.
2. **Compact Representation**: Hexadecimal (base 16) is much shorter than binary for representing the same value.
